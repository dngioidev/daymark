## Web
Backlog holds ideas without a date. They stay out of Today, the calendar, daily counts and old-task warnings until you pick them. Open Backlog from the side menu, or choose the Backlog target in New task. Give the idea a title and, optionally, a description and category. Category suggestions include existing labels and section names.

Use Pick a day when you are ready. Choose today or a future date. A matching section is reused; otherwise Daymark creates a section named after the category. Unlabelled ideas use the default section. Edit, reorder within a category, or delete ideas with Undo. Move to backlog in an open task's menu returns dated work to this waiting area.
## Mobile
Tap Backlog in the bottom bar to see undated ideas grouped by category. You can add one there or tap plus, switch New task to Backlog, and enter a title with an optional category. These ideas do not age, carry forward or appear in daily progress. Keep immediate work on Today and later possibilities here.

When an idea becomes actionable, use its Pick a day control and choose today or a future day. Its category determines the destination section, creating that section when necessary. You can edit, reorder or delete an idea from its row. To park an existing open task, use Move to backlog from the task's menu. Done tasks cannot be parked this way.
