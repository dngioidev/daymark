## Web
1. Open **Backlog** from the side menu, or choose the Backlog target in New task.
2. Give the idea a title and, if you like, a description and category.
3. When you are ready, use **Pick a day** and choose today or a future date.

Backlog ideas stay out of Today, the calendar, daily counts and old-task warnings. When you pick a day, a matching section is reused; otherwise Daymark creates one named after the category. Edit, reorder or delete ideas with Undo.
## Phone
1. Tap **Backlog** in the bottom bar.
2. Add an idea there, or tap plus and switch New task to Backlog.
3. When it becomes actionable, tap **Pick a day** and choose a date.

Ideas do not age, carry forward or appear in daily progress. To park an existing open task, use **Move to backlog** from its menu.
