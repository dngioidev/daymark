## Web
1. Set your usual section names in **Settings → Sections**, or add a section to the current day.
2. Open a task's menu and choose **Move to section**.
3. Pick a section, or type a new name and press Enter. Undo is offered.

The task moves to the end of that section and keeps its history. **Move to date** reschedules work; **Move to backlog** parks an open task outside the calendar. A task's status, set under **Settings → Statuses**, controls how unfinished work behaves at rollover.
## Phone
1. Tap a task's menu.
2. Choose **Move to section**. The current section is disabled.
3. Tap another section, or type a name under New section and tap Move.

The task keeps its details and history, and an Undo action appears. Use **Move to date** for another day and **Move to backlog** for work that should wait. Status options come from **Settings → Statuses**.
