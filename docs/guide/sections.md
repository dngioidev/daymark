title: Sections, statuses and moving tasks
order: 3
---
## Web
Sections organise tasks within a day. Set your usual section names in Settings, or add a section to the current day. A task's status controls how unfinished work behaves at rollover; configure those rules in Settings under Statuses. Checking a task marks it done without requiring you to change its section.

To reorganise a task, open its menu and choose Move to section. Pick an existing section or enter a new name and press Enter. Matching names ignore case and extra surrounding spaces. The task moves to the end of that section and keeps its identity and history. Undo is offered. Move to date reschedules work; Move to backlog parks an open task outside the calendar.
## Mobile
Tap a task's menu to open its actions. Move to section lists sections from that task's day, with the current section disabled. Tap another section, or type a name under New section and use Move. The task keeps its details and history. An Undo action appears after the move; use it promptly, before making further edits.

Use Move to date when the task belongs on another day. Use Move to backlog for unfinished work that should wait without a date. Status options come from Settings under Statuses and can affect whether unfinished tasks carry forward. A completed task can change section, while a historical closed copy cannot be moved again through this action.
