## Web
Calendar helps you review past work and plan future days. Use month navigation to find a date, then open a day to view its notebook. The month grid summarises activity, while the week view lists tasks in readable day rows. Existing controls switch between these views without changing the underlying notes.

Create future work by choosing a date in New task or by moving an existing task to a date. On desktop, supported drag actions can also reschedule tasks. Review the destination after a move and use Undo if needed. Backlog ideas do not appear on Calendar until picked into a day; they have no scheduled date to display.
## Mobile
Tap Calendar in the bottom bar to browse your days. Use the month arrows to navigate and tap a day to open its notebook. Switch to the week view when you want the tasks listed by day instead of a compact month grid. The layout stacks day rows to keep their titles readable on a phone.

To schedule work, tap plus and choose its date. An existing task can be rescheduled through its Move to date action; there is no need to drag precisely on a small screen. Backlog ideas stay outside the calendar until you choose Pick a day. Calendar is a view of your daily notebooks, not a separate copy of their data.
