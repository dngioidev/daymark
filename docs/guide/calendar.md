## Web
1. Open **Calendar** and use the month arrows to find a date.
2. Open a day to view its notebook.
3. Switch to **Week** to read tasks in day rows, or **Events week** for a separate seven-day time grid. Events week uses the device's current time zone and keeps the original scheduled date visible when a zone shift places it on a different local day. **Today** returns to the current device-local week.

Create future work by choosing a date in New task or with **Move to date**. On desktop you can also drag tasks to reschedule them; check the destination and use Undo if needed. Backlog ideas appear only once you pick a day for them.
## Phone
1. Tap **Calendar** in the bottom bar.
2. Use the month arrows and tap a day to open its notebook.
3. Switch to **Week** to see tasks listed by day, or **Events week** to scan scheduled times. Swipe horizontally to see later days; the time gutter and day headings stay visible while scrolling.

To schedule work, tap plus and choose its date, or use **Move to date** on an existing task. Calendar shows your daily notebooks and the events assigned to each original-zone calendar date; it is not a separate copy of their data. Events week projects timed events into device-local days without changing their saved date, time or time zone. A multi-day event continues through each occupied date, but an event ending exactly at midnight does not continue onto the next date. Commitments with a duration but no start time are listed as **No set time**. Event counts are separate from task counts, and dates with events remain selectable without a task document.
