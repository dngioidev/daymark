## Web
1. Open **Calendar** and use the month arrows to find a date.
2. Open a day to view its notebook.
3. Switch to the week view to read tasks in day rows.

Create future work by choosing a date in New task or with **Move to date**. On desktop you can also drag tasks to reschedule them; check the destination and use Undo if needed. Backlog ideas appear only once you pick a day for them.
## Phone
1. Tap **Calendar** in the bottom bar.
2. Use the month arrows and tap a day to open its notebook.
3. Switch to the week view to see tasks listed by day.

To schedule work, tap plus and choose its date, or use **Move to date** on an existing task. Calendar shows your daily notebooks; it is not a separate copy of their data.
