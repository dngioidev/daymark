## Web
1. Open **Calendar** and use the month arrows to find a date.
2. Open a day to view its notebook.
3. Switch to the week view to read tasks in day rows. Events appear in a separate agenda for the selected day.

Create future work by choosing a date in New task or with **Move to date**. On desktop you can also drag tasks to reschedule them; check the destination and use Undo if needed. Backlog ideas appear only once you pick a day for them.
## Phone
1. Tap **Calendar** in the bottom bar.
2. Use the month arrows and tap a day to open its notebook.
3. Switch to the week view to see tasks listed by day. The selected day's event agenda is separate from task totals.

To schedule work, tap plus and choose its date, or use **Move to date** on an existing task. Calendar shows your daily notebooks and the events assigned to each original-zone calendar date; it is not a separate copy of their data. A multi-day event continues through each occupied date, but an event ending exactly at midnight does not continue onto the next date. Commitments with a duration but no start time are listed as **No set time**. Event counts are separate from task counts, and dates with events remain selectable without a task document.
