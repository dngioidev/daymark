## Web
Home-screen widgets are provided by the Android app. A desktop browser or installed web app does not expose the native Android widgets. If you want tasks or habits visible on your phone's home screen, install the Android APK, open it, and connect the same sync server used by your browser.

The Android task and habits widgets use the app's local data. Let the app sync when checking something recently changed on the desktop. Widget settings control their appearance and content where supported. The backlog has no widget: pick an idea into a day before expecting it among daily tasks. Keep the Android app installed and its data intact for its widgets to function.
## Mobile
Install and open the Android app before adding a widget. Long-press an empty area of your home screen, open your launcher's widget picker, and find Daymark. Add the task or habits widget, then use the available widget settings to choose the supported display options. Exact placement and resizing controls depend on the launcher.

Widgets read the app's local data and can queue supported actions for the app. Open Daymark and let sync finish when you need recent changes from another device. A phone browser installation does not provide these native widgets. Backlog ideas have no widget; pick them into a day to make them daily work. Removing app data or uninstalling also affects the widgets.
