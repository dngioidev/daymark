## Web
The editor supports headings, lists, checkboxes, tables and code; content stays Markdown. Paste pictures, use Mermaid code blocks for diagrams, and type double brackets to link notes.

Notes has one column: Search, New note, New folder, List | Graph, then a folder tree. Pinned notes sit on top. Search filters the tree and dims an open note that does not match. The selected folder is where new notes and folders land. Drag notes into folders or the Move to top level strip. Arrow keys, Home, End and Enter navigate; Shift+F10 opens folder actions. Deleting a folder moves its notes up. Below 1100 pixels, Notes opens a drawer.

The Related panel suggests notes: Link adds a link, Not related hides a suggestion; Restore under Hidden suggestions brings it back. Graph shows connections with folder and tag filters.

Full screen (the Full screen button, or Ctrl/⌘ + .) hides the title, folder row, Related, footer and navigation. A typed note keeps only the editor and its formatting bar. Exit full screen or Escape returns.

Open Note templates beside New note, choose Handwritten note, then lined, grid or blank paper. Choose Pen, Highlighter or Eraser (area or whole-stroke). A pen draws on any device; mouse drawing works on a device where no pen has been used. Fingers scroll and never draw. Hold the S Pen button while writing to erase; release it to get your tool back.

In full screen, a handwritten note shows a side rail with every tool, colours, Undo, Redo, pages and Exit. More writing options > Move rail to right remembers this device's choice.

Select: draw a loop around strokes or text boxes, drag inside the box to move, drag a corner to resize. The action bar offers Delete, Recolor selection, Copy, Cut, Paste and Duplicate. Click outside to clear.

Text boxes work without a pen: choose Text, click the page and type; click away to save, click a box to edit it. Draw-and-hold shapes: draw a line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse or triangle and keep the pen still at the end to tidy it. Quick strokes stay freehand, and Undo brings the freehand stroke back. Handwriting search is title only. Sync and ZIP or JSON backups keep ink and text boxes; Markdown export contains a placeholder.

On a typed note, turn on Highlighter and choose a colour, then drag the pen across words. Highlights stay with the words through edits and sync. Turn it off for ordinary pen behaviour; mouse and finger drags never highlight.
## Mobile
Tap Notes in the bottom bar. Search and the folder tree open first. Select a note to edit; Back returns to the column. Formatting supports headings, lists, checkboxes and Markdown. Pictures and Mermaid diagrams work too; double-bracket links offer suggestions.

Folders nest. Tap a folder to expand it. Its actions create, rename, move or delete folders; deletion moves notes up. Move to folder files an open note. The Related button opens suggestions: Link adds a link, Not related hides a pair; Restore under Hidden suggestions brings it back. Graph needs a tablet or wider screen.

Full screen (the Full screen button) hides the title, Move to folder, Related, footer and navigation. Typed notes keep the editor and formatting bar; handwritten notes show a side rail with every tool, Undo, Redo, pages and Exit. Exit full screen or Android Back returns. More writing options > Move rail to right remembers this device's choice.

For a handwritten note, open Note templates beside New note and choose Handwritten note, then lined, grid or blank paper. Choose Pen, Highlighter or Eraser (area or whole-stroke). Hold the S Pen button while writing to erase; release it to get your tool back. Pen pressure changes line width; palms and fingers do not draw. On a device where no pen has been used, mouse drawing works. Finger scrolling on writable pages has no momentum; pinch zoom is off there.

Select: draw a loop around strokes or text boxes, drag inside the box to move, drag a corner to resize. The action bar offers Delete, Recolor selection, Copy, Cut, Paste and Duplicate. Tap outside to clear.

Text boxes work without a pen: choose Text, tap the page and type; tap away to save, tap a box to edit it. Draw-and-hold shapes: draw a line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse or triangle and keep the pen still at the end to tidy it. Quick strokes stay freehand, and Undo brings the freehand stroke back. Handwriting search is title only; ink and text boxes sync and are included in ZIP and JSON backups.

For typed notes, tap Highlighter, choose a colour and drag the S Pen across words. Highlights follow later edits and sync to other devices. Turn the tool off to select text normally.
