## Web
1. Open **Notes** and choose **New note**, or a template from **Note templates**.
2. Write with headings, lists, checkboxes, tables, code, pictures and Mermaid diagrams. The note stays Markdown.
3. Choose **New folder**, then drag notes into folders or onto **Move to top level**.
4. Use the pin button to keep a note at the top of the list.

The selected folder is where new notes and folders land. Deleting a folder moves its notes up. In the tree, arrow keys, Home, End and Enter move around; Shift+F10 opens folder actions. Below 1100 pixels wide, Notes opens as a drawer.

On a typed note, turn on **Highlighter**, choose a colour and drag the pen across words. Mouse and finger drags never highlight.
## Phone
1. Tap **Notes** in the bottom bar. Search and the folder tree open first.
2. Tap **New note**, or tap a note to edit it; Back returns to the list.
3. Tap a folder to expand it. Its actions create, rename, move or delete folders.
4. Use **Move to folder** to file the open note, and the pin button to keep it on top.

For typed notes, tap **Highlighter**, choose a colour and drag the S Pen across words. Highlights follow later edits and sync.
