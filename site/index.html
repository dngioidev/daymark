Your day, in plain Markdown. Unfinished tasks carry forward.

Daymark is a local-first daily notebook. Each day is a Markdown note; whatever you didn't finish moves to today with its history, and tasks that keep slipping get a gentle warning. Habits, a calendar to plan ahead, notes and home-screen widgets, on the web and on Android.

Download the Android app Release notes

The APK comes from the GitHub release. The web app runs on a server you host yourself.

What it does

Ideas can wait

Keep undated tasks in Backlog with category labels. Pick a day when you are ready; until then they stay out of daily counts and warnings.

A side menu with room to write

Desktop navigation stays beside your work, on the left or right. Notes focus mode hides it. Move tasks between sections without retyping them.

Tasks that carry forward

Unfinished tasks move to today automatically, keeping their history. Finish one late and Daymark records the day it was really done, and closes its older copies.

Daily habits

Set your habits once and tick them each day. Streaks, a warning when one slips for too long, and a habits widget on your home screen.

Calendar to plan ahead

Month and week views with every task of the day. Open any day, plan future ones, and drag tasks to reschedule.

Notes with room to write

A rich editor with tables, synced pictures, Mermaid diagrams and [[links]] with autocomplete. Paste from AI chats to keep headings, lists and code. Templates and focus mode give you room to write.

One column for your notes

Folders and the notes inside them live in a single column with search that filters in place, pinned notes on top, drag and drop, and a Move to folder picker on phones. Use Minimize notes column for the shortcut strip and Expand notes column to return. Folders export as real directories.

Related notes, one click away

While you write, Daymark suggests notes that share distinctive words. Link adds a [[wiki link]]; Not related hides the pair. Suggestions are worked out on your device.

A graph of your notes

See links, suggestions and tags as a knowledge graph. Filter by folder or tag, focus on one note's neighbourhood, or use the keyboard-friendly list view.

Make it yours

Light or dark, your own colours, background photos and text size, all kept readable.

Yours to keep

Plain Markdown files you can export at any time, offline by default, no telemetry. Sync between your devices through a server you run.

Get Daymark

Android

Install the APK from the latest release ({{VERSION}}). It includes the task and habits home-screen widgets. Installing over an older version keeps your notes and sign-in.

Web app and sync server

Run the server with Docker and open it in any browser; it installs as an app and works offline. Your phone and computers sync through it.
